The decision rule. Gain: the low end of the 95% range of the gain in attack-class recall must be above 5 points. False positives: the false-positive rate may rise by at most 1 point over isolated training. Privacy (sharing): if the gain is met but the organization's own sent records are traced above the majority-host baseline, the result is a trade-off. Data sent is reported, not judged. Verdict words of the rule: not worth it (the gain is not met, or the false-positive rate rises by more than 1 point); trade-off (sanitized attack-record sharing: the gain is met and the organization's own sent records are traced above the baseline); worth it (sanitized attack-record sharing: the gain is met and they are not); gain met (federated learning).
