THE RUN
Six stages, from the file to the scorecard

Every stage shows what the product did to the data and why. A quick look takes well under a minute on most samples.

1

2
Detect column roles
3

4

5
Train and test
6
Scorecard
1
Ingest

The file is read into a table. Records and columns are counted; nothing is changed or stored.

2
Detect column roles

The label, the value that means normal, the host column, the clock and the identifiers are proposed, each with its reason.

3
Sanitize

Identifiers are removed. The behavior numbers and the attack type stay. One record is shown before and after.

4
Organize

Hosts are split into organizations A, B and C, one attack type is withheld from each, and every label is mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.

5
Train and test

The same detector is trained under the four training conditions and tested on the withheld attack type.

6
Scorecard

Gains with 95% ranges, false-positive rates, data sent, the tracing test and the verdicts of the decision rule.
